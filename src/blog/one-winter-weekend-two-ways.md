Winter is the quiet season in St. Catharines and the Benchlands, and that is the point. The wineries have time to talk. The restaurants have tables. The lights are on in every window. Here is the same weekend planned two ways. [PLACEHOLDER: client to confirm names and hours.]

## The slow way: wine country in the snow

**Friday.** Check in at [Inn On The Twenty](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/) in Jordan Village before dark. Walk Main Street while the shop windows glow, then eat at the inn or book the tasting menu at [Restaurant Pearl Morissette](https://restaurantpearlmorissette.com/), Ontario's only two-MICHELIN-Star restaurant, ten minutes up the road.

**Saturday.** Sleep in. Breakfast at [RPM Bakehouse](https://rpmbakehouse.com/), where the breads are made from heritage grains and the coffee is excellent. Spend the afternoon on the Beamsville Bench: a fireside tasting at [Fielding Estates](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/), icewine at [PLACEHOLDER winery], and the Escarpment view from [Megalomaniac](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/) as the snow turns blue. Dinner is a short drive back to the village.

**Sunday.** A walk at Ball's Falls, where the two waterfalls freeze into columns, then a long lunch at a winery dining room before the drive home. Seventy-five minutes to Toronto.

## The fast way: a downtown night out

**Friday.** Stay downtown in St. Catharines so nobody drives. Start with cocktails at [Wandering Spirits](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/), then dinner at [Fat Rabbit](https://fat-rabbit.ca/) or its louder little sibling [Bar Les Incompétents](https://www.barlesincompetents.ca/), a Bib Gourmand with a raw bar worth fighting over.

**Saturday.** Brunch at [OddBird](https://oddbird.ca/), then the holiday shops on St. Paul Street and the [Farmers Market](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/) for baking to take home. In the afternoon, skate at [PLACEHOLDER rink] under the lights. Catch a show at the [FirstOntario Performing Arts Centre](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/) and finish with a late plate downtown.

**Sunday.** Drive twenty minutes to Port Dalhousie for a cold walk on the pier and a warm breakfast, then home.

Either way, you leave with a plan for next time. See [where to eat and drink](/eatdrinkplay/) and [where to stay](/eatdrinkplay/#accommodations).
