Every town in St. Catharines and the Benchlands lights up for the season, and most of it is free. Here is one evening that strings the best of it together. [PLACEHOLDER: client to confirm the 2026 displays, dates and hours.]

## Start downtown at dusk

The lights on St. Paul Street and in Montebello Park come on in late November and stay on through the holidays. Park once and walk. The shop windows are decorated, the candy trail runs on weekends in December, and there is hot chocolate on every block. If the rink at [PLACEHOLDER rink] is open, this is the time to skate: the lights are on and the crowds are thin before dinner.

## Dinner that kids like

[OddBird](https://oddbird.ca/) is relaxed enough for families and good enough for parents. For something faster, the [PLACEHOLDER pizza place] on St. Paul Street does a kids' menu, and the [Farmers Market](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/) building has warm food stalls on market days.

## Then out to the falls

Twenty minutes west, Ball's Falls runs lantern walks through its historic village on December weekends [PLACEHOLDER dates]. The path is flat, the barn is warm, and the two waterfalls freeze into something kids remember. Bring boots.

## Or the waterfront

Port Dalhousie decorates Lakeside Park and the carousel pavilion. A short cold walk on the pier, a look at the lighthouse, and back to the car. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm the Port Dalhousie Christmas Market date.]

## Where the wineries join in

Several Benchlands wineries string their vines and open late on December weekends, with fire pits outside and hot cider inside. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm which wineries run light displays this year.] They are a good last stop on the drive home.
