The farms that sell peaches in August sell trees and pies in December. Here is a Saturday in St. Catharines and the Benchlands built around them. [PLACEHOLDER: client to confirm farms, markets and dates.]

## Morning: cut the tree

The tree farms along the bench between Vineland and Beamsville open on weekends from late November. At [PLACEHOLDER Vineland Tree Farm] you take a saw and a wagon into the rows, pick a fir, and the farm nets it and ties it to the roof. There is cider in the barn and usually a bonfire. Go early; the best trees go by noon.

## Lunch: a farm market

[Peach Country Farm Market](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/) and [Romagnoli Family Farm](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/) stay open through the holidays with soup, pies, preserves and a greenhouse full of poinsettias. Buy the pie for tonight.

## Afternoon: the market

Jordan Village runs a holiday market on Main Street on the last weekend of November [PLACEHOLDER date], with local makers, mulled wine for the grown-ups and carols. In St. Catharines, the [Farmers Market](https://www.wanderniagara.com/directory/) downtown has holiday baking on Saturdays, and Port Dalhousie's Christmas Market lights the carousel pavilion in early December [PLACEHOLDER date].

## Late afternoon: a walk and the lights

If there is daylight left, Ball's Falls is twenty minutes away and the trail to the lower falls is flat and short. Then back into downtown St. Catharines for the lights on St. Paul Street and dinner that kids like.
